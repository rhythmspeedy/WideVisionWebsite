About Us

We blend artistic storytelling with technical execution to shape unforgettable worlds.

WideVision VFX Studio is a visual effects company that specializes in creating stunning digital imagery for films and other media. Working with clients from around the world, we bring their visions to life through advanced techniques and technology.

WideVision VFX Studio is headquarted with a head branch in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We have a secondary branch in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

From the heart of Tamil Nadu, we serve clients across the entire world with beautiful visual effects.

When we first started this company, we wanted to expand across the entire world of visual effects, including rotomachinery, preparatory work, paint work, 3d tracking, and camera tracking. Our current goals involve expanding to more vfx services and building a larger team.
